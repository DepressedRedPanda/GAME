# Local mercenary jobs — narrative outlines

## 01 — The Missing Cells (first vertical slice)
**Client:** Oren Bale, freight clerk. **Rumor:** crates from his company were stolen.
1. Speak to Oren at the job board. He offers 60 chits to recover a power-cell crate.
2. Investigate wagon grooves beside the east gate and follow them to the Old Relay.
3. Bandits are using the cells to keep their village water pump going. Choose assault, sneak, or parley.
4. Return crate to Oren (+60 chits, +100 XP, Oren approval), leave cells with bandits (+35 XP, village's future favor), or steal cells (+sale value, faction tension).
5. The job changes who trades with you, but does NOT gate the rest of the game.

## 02 — A Thimble of Mercy
**Client:** Nima Voss, field medic. Gather medgel and clean cloth to craft two healing wraps. Reward: a restorative injector and +70 XP. Teaches scavenging and crafting.

## 03 — Nine Bells and a Body
**Client:** Tessa Rhun, tavern keeper. Find an absent debt collector, examine a staged crime scene, interrogate two witnesses, decide whether to reveal the perpetrator. Reward depends on evidence and choice, not body count.

## Character intentions
**Oren Bale:** exploits supply shortages, but not entirely dishonest.
**Nima Voss:** sees the android as a person rather than a tool.
**Tessa Rhun:** knows everyone and sells silence.
**Silt Jack:** outlaw leader looking after displaced miners.

## Rule
Player choice creates local consequences, not a sprawling simulated galaxy. Every option needs a practical implementation and a test before it enters the milestone.
