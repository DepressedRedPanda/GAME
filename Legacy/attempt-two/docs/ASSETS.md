# Free content and compatibility policy

**Engine pin:** Unreal 5.8.3 (5.8 family) for first build. Free content availability is tied to asset compatibility and license, not simply to selecting an older engine.

## Included by Epic's base Third Person template
- Manny/Quinn skeletal mannequins, movement animation graphs, starter geometry and Enhanced Input mappings. Choose **Combat** variant for an immediately playable melee/targeting foundation.
- UE engine primitive meshes for the graybox town.

## Optional official assets to acquire in your own Fab account
- Game Animation Sample Project (locomotion/retargeting study).
- Quixel / Fab free environment materials and Megascans appropriate to alien desert/dusty frontier.
- Other free Epic samples and locomotion assets only after reviewing per-asset UE5.8 compatibility and license.

Links: https://www.unrealengine.com/fabfreecontent ; https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/third-person-template-in-unreal-engine ; https://www.fab.com/

## Not silently bundled
- Paid marketplace packs; generic 'free' packs with unconfirmed licenses.
- A gun/sword mesh, jacket, or MetaHuman: none has been licensed/downloaded/rigged in this repo.
- Lyra modules: Lyra contains C++ and is much heavier than the small Blueprint game requested.

## Asset acceptance tests
For each third-party asset log its Fab listing URL, license, exact supported engine versions, skeleton/IK retarget requirement, approximate download size, and whether source can be redistributed in GitHub. Favor one shared skeleton for weapon animation and clothing over photorealism first.
