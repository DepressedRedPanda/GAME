# Creative direction — HEAVY METTLE

## North star
Play a human-looking amnesiac android trying to be useful in a remote world that doesn't understand him. Play up the mundane: unpaid tabs, petty grievances, the bandit with a sick mother, missing shipments, bad trades, village gossip. The frontier should feel inhabited before any grand lore is unveiled.

## Genre
Third-person shooter meets compact action RPG. Tonally a spaghetti western: long quiet walks, precarious alliances, ugly dust, sudden violence, and moral choices that have local consequences. No spells, fantasy mana or ancient gods powering the mechanics: use battery power, industrial chemistry, prosthetics and salvage tech.

## The player
- Humanlike male-presenting android, apparently about nineteen or twenty, more lost youth than invincible terminator.
- A long weathered duster coat with Chaos Cloth simulation (art/rigging task, not shipped).
- Sword across back, sidearm visibly holstered at right thigh (socket attachments).
- Combat modes: 3.5m maximum proximity lock-on for melee; over that distance rely on shoulder-camera free aiming.
- Health = structural damage, stamina = exertion, battery = short-duration tech abilities. No magic.

## Location: Cinder's Rest
A small fortified market settlement on the edge of red salt flats. Weathered stone, timber, canvas awnings, repurposed satellite panels and scavenged solar batteries. Most residents are human/alien hybrids; their appearance, culture and economics should reflect actual daily needs, not a monolithic 'alien' aesthetic.

## Proposed first-playable 15-minute loop
Arrive at the town gate -> ask about mercenary work -> accept Missing Cells -> track crate footprints to badlands -> fight or sneak around two raiders -> collect salvage and delivery -> return for payment -> buy consumables/craft ammunition -> unlock first character upgrade.

## Scope discipline
One village, one nearby combat location, three NPCs, two enemy types, one handgun, one sword, one simple crafting bench, one full quest, one save slot. Expand only after this 15-minute loop is solid.
